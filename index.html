Your pricing formula

Target revenue = Total RRP + (RRP × uplift %) + (stream hours × hourly wages)

For a mixer, add the RRPs of all products. Case breaks work the same way: quantity × RRP.

Upload a checklist

Give each checklist a title and format. You can save multiple formats from the same release, such as Pick Your Driver, Pick Your Team, and combined PYD/PYT.

Recognised columns include Spot / Name / Driver / Team, Category, Score / Weight, Tier, Reference Price and Notes. A simple one-name-per-line list also works. Country/team lines with Auto, Numbered and Insert counts are recognised automatically and receive editable suggested scores (not real hit odds). Excel import requires the included online spreadsheet library.

2 · Price target & profit

Every spot is given a simple value tier. More valuable tiers get a bigger portion of the revenue target. You can also enter a custom score from 0–100 for any spot.

Stream discounts & profit forecast

Assumes every active spot sells at the same discount. Shows the amount collected and profit after total RRP and wages, before fees or postage. Negative profit means the break would fall short.
